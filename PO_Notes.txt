


Features
-> Guests
	- Book a flight with instructor
	- Rent a plane
-> Students
	- Book a flight with instructor
	- Book a flight without instructor
	- Schedule a ground class with instructor
-> Instructors
-> Staff
	- Charge for flight
	- View all reservations
	- Make a reservation
-> Admin
	- Reports for all revenue & expenditure
-> Common
	- User creation
	- Roles management
	- Integration with Google
